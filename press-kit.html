Bean Boom is a free online minesweeper game with a twist: correctly flagged mines detonate chain reactions, and quick reveals build combo multipliers up to 4x.
Bean Boom reinvents the classic minesweeper puzzle for the score-chasing generation. Two modes are included: Egg Mode adds a full scoring system with combo multipliers, FEVER bonuses, and chain-reaction blasts triggered by correct flags, while Classic Mode offers pure traditional minesweeper with global speed leaderboards. Play free in your browser on desktop or mobile - no download, no signup. Skill-based challenges let dedicated players win back their entry fee on completion.

Key Features

  1. Two game modes - Egg Mode (arcade scoring) and Classic Mode (traditional timed play)
  2. Chain reaction blasts - correct flags detonate mines, clearing surrounding cells and chaining into bigger explosions
  3. Combo & FEVER system - fast reveals build multipliers up to 3x; a 20-combo triggers 4x FEVER mode
  4. Global leaderboards - score boards (Egg) and speed boards (Classic) for three difficulties, verified server-side
  5. Skill-based challenges - optional paid challenges with automatic full refund upon completion (not gambling: outcomes verified by server-side replay)
  6. Installable PWA - works offline after first load, installable on mobile and desktop
  7. 8 languages - including right-to-left Arabic support
