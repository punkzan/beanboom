How the Daily Leaderboard Works

The daily minesweeper leaderboard resets every day, giving every player a fresh shot at the top. Win a game on any difficulty and submit your result — you'll instantly see your daily rank. Daily boards are the most active competition: even a one-minute Easy game can land you a podium spot on a quiet day.

Monthly Competition & Yearly Championship

The monthly competition tracks the best results across the whole month, and in Classic Mode the yearly championship crowns the fastest players of the year on each difficulty. Climb high enough and your name carries across periods — a single great game can sit on the daily, monthly, yearly, and all-time boards at once.

Fair Play: Server-Side Verification

Every submitted result — time or score — is verified by server-side replay: the server re-plays your entire game log move by move and confirms the result is legitimate before it enters the leaderboard. Modified clients, scripted inputs, and impossible times are rejected automatically. The boards you see are earned.

How to Get on the Boards

  1. Open Bean Boom — free, no download, no registration.
  2. Pick Classic Mode (for times) or Egg Mode (for scores) and a difficulty.
  3. Win the game — only completed wins can be submitted.
  4. Enter your name and submit. Your result is verified and ranked instantly.
  5. Check the daily, monthly, yearly, and all-time tabs to see where you stand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to appear on the leaderboard?

No. Just enter a display name when you submit your result. No registration or email required.

Do both game modes share one leaderboard?

No. Classic Mode has time-based leaderboards and Egg Mode has score-based leaderboards, each with separate boards for Easy, Medium, and Hard.

Can I improve my ranking?

Yes. Daily and monthly boards always take your best result within the period, so keep playing — a better win replaces your slower entry.

When do the leaderboards reset?

Daily boards reset every day, monthly boards at the start of each month, and yearly boards on January 1st. The all-time boards never reset.
