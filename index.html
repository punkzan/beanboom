Bean Boom - Free Online Minesweeper Game

Bean Boom is a free online minesweeper game with a unique bead art style. Inspired by classic minesweeper puzzles and perler bead craft, it brings the timeless logic game to your browser with colorful visuals, three difficulty levels, and a global leaderboard. No download and no registration are required to start playing.

How to Play Minesweeper Online

  1. Reveal tiles by clicking or tapping cells on the board.
  2. Each number tells you how many mines are hidden in the eight adjacent cells.
  3. Flag suspected mines with right-click on desktop or long-press on mobile.
  4. Clear every safe cell without hitting a mine to win the game.

Game Features

  • Three difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard
  • Global leaderboard with daily, monthly, and all-time rankings
  • Unique bead art style inspired by perler bead craft

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bean Boom free to play?

Yes. The core minesweeper game is completely free. Optional paid challenges are available if you want to compete for rewards.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Bean Boom runs directly in your browser. Just open the page and start playing.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Bean Boom works on both desktop and mobile browsers, with touch controls for revealing and flagging tiles.

How does the global leaderboard work?

Your best times are submitted to the server after each game. Compete with players around the world across daily, monthly, and all-time rankings.
