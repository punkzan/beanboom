## 5. Maker Comment（发布后 1 小时内贴出）

> Hey Product Hunt! 👋
>
> I'm a solo dev, and Bean Boom is my take on a question that bugged me for years: minesweeper is a brilliant logic game, so why is it stuck in 1990?
>
> So I added the one thing it never had — **consequences for playing well**. In Egg Mode:
>
> 🚩 Flag a mine correctly → it **detonates**, clearing cells around it
> 💥 Detonations **chain** into bigger explosions
> ⚡ Quick reveals build a **combo multiplier** up to 3x (4x in FEVER mode)
> 🏆 Winning banks your score to global, server-verified leaderboards
>
> Prefer the original? **Classic Mode** is pure minesweeper with speed leaderboards — no powerups, just you and the timer.
>
> Everything runs free in your browser, no signup, installable as a PWA, available in 8 languages. There's also a full tutorial library on the site (from "what do the numbers mean" to 1-2-1 patterns) — it's genuinely free content, no email wall.
>
> I'd love feedback on the difficulty curve and the challenge system. What's your minesweeper record? 🎮
