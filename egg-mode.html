Egg Mode: Minesweeper with a Scoring System

Chain reactions, combo multipliers, and FEVER mode — minesweeper like you've never played it.

Egg Mode is Bean Boom's signature take on classic minesweeper. Instead of just racing the clock, every cell you reveal earns points — and the faster and smarter you play, the higher your score climbs. It's a minesweeper game with a scoring system built for players who love chasing high scores.

How the Scoring System Works

Points in Egg Mode come from four sources that stack together:

1. Reveal Points

Every safe cell you uncover earns base points. Clear a large empty region in one click and every cell counts.

2. Combo Multipliers

Reveal cells in quick succession to build a combo. Each consecutive reveal within 3 seconds increases your multiplier — up to 3x in normal play. Milestone combos flash on screen: Nice (5), Great (10), Amazing (15), and FEVER (20).

3. Chain Reaction Blasts

This is where the name Bean Boom comes from. Correctly flag a mine and it doesn't just sit there — it detonates, blasting away the safe cells around it. Blasted mines chain into further explosions, and every cell caught in the chain-boom scores bonus points. One well-placed flag can clear half the board.

4. FEVER Mode

Reach a 20-combo and FEVER activates: your multiplier cap rises to 4x and every blast gets a wider explosion radius. FEVER is the key to record-breaking scores — keep the combo alive while it lasts.

Tips for High Scores

  1. Play fast, not frantic. Combos expire after 3 idle seconds — keep a steady rhythm instead of freezing to think.
  2. Save flags for clusters. Flagging a mine surrounded by other mines triggers the biggest chain reactions.
  3. Chain into FEVER. When your combo is at 15+, prioritize quick reveals over flagging to hit the 20-combo FEVER threshold.
  4. Blast during FEVER. Blasts get +1 radius in FEVER mode — detonating then clears more cells per boom.
  5. Finish the board. Your score only counts if you win, and finishing quickly adds a time bonus multiplier.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Egg Mode free to play?

Yes. Both Egg Mode and Classic Mode are completely free, played instantly in your browser with no download or registration.

Do I need to flag mines to score points?

Flagging is optional for winning, but correctly flagged mines trigger chain-boom blasts — one of the biggest score sources in Egg Mode.

How do I activate FEVER mode?

Build a combo of 20 consecutive reveals, each within 3 seconds of the last. FEVER then raises your multiplier cap to 4x until the combo breaks.

Can I play Egg Mode on mobile?

Yes. Tap to reveal, long-press to flag. The combo and scoring system work exactly the same on touch devices.
