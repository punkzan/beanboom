Classic Mode: Pure Minesweeper, No Powerups

The timeless logic game exactly as you remember it — just you, the grid, and the clock.

Classic Mode is classic minesweeper, free online, untouched. No scoring system, no chain reactions, no powerups — reveal every safe cell as fast as you can without detonating a mine. Your completion time is the only thing that matters.

What Makes It "Classic"

Pure Minesweeper, No Powerups

Every mine stays a mine — flag it or work around it. Correctly flagged mines don't detonate, cells don't blast, and there are no bonus points. If you want the pure, unmodified minesweeper experience, this is it.

Classic Rules

Numbers tell you how many mines hide in the eight adjacent cells. Right-click (or long-press) to flag. First click is always safe. Win by revealing every safe cell on the board.

Three Standard Difficulties

Easy (9×9, 10 mines), Medium (16×16, 40 mines), and Hard (16×30, 99 mines) — the same board sizes the world has played since the 1990s. See the difficulty guide for details.

Paid Challenges

Classic Mode is also home to paid challenges: pay a small entry fee, and win it back — with a bonus — by completing the challenge goal (for example, winning a set number of games). It's a skill-based test of consistency, not luck. Challenges are optional; the core game is completely free.

Classic Mode vs Egg Mode

Curious about the other side? Egg Mode adds a scoring system with combo multipliers, chain-reaction blasts, and FEVER mode, with score-based leaderboards. Classic Mode stays pure; Egg Mode goes wild. Switch between them anytime — the game remembers your choice.

Tips for Faster Times

  1. Learn the patterns. Most minesweeper positions resolve with known patterns like 1-1 and 1-2 — recognizing them instantly is where speed comes from. Start with our patterns guide.
  2. Flag less. You win by revealing safe cells, not flagging mines. Only flag when the flag unlocks safe cells you couldn't otherwise confirm.
  3. Use both buttons (chording). Click a satisfied number with both mouse buttons (or double-tap on mobile) to reveal all its remaining neighbors at once.
  4. Play in streaks. Start your run in the center where numbers give the most information, and cascade outward.
  5. Watch the leaders. Check the leaderboard to see what times you need to beat on each difficulty.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Classic Minesweeper really free?

Yes. Classic Mode is completely free with unlimited plays, no download, and no registration. Paid challenges are optional extras.

What's the difference between Classic Mode and Egg Mode?

Classic Mode is pure minesweeper ranked by completion time. Egg Mode adds a scoring system with combos, chain-reaction blasts, and FEVER mode, ranked by score. Both are free.

Are the leaderboards fair?

Yes. Every submitted time is verified by server-side replay of the full game log, so impossible or modified times are rejected automatically.

Does Classic Mode work on mobile?

Yes. Tap to reveal, long-press to flag, and double-tap a number to chord — full touch support in any mobile browser.
