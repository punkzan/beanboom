Privacy Policy

How Bean Boom handles your data.

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bean Boom ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our free online minesweeper game at bb.superzan.net (the "Service"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information We Store Locally

When you play Bean Boom, the following data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage:

  • Game settings (difficulty, sound preference, language)
  • Best times for each difficulty level
  • Account credentials (username and password hash, if you create an account)
  • Display name and country/region (if provided)

This data never leaves your device unless you submit a score to the leaderboard. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage.

Leaderboard Data

When you complete a game and choose to submit your score to the global leaderboard, the following information is sent to our server:

  • Your display name (as entered by you)
  • Your country/region (as entered by you)
  • Your completion time
  • The difficulty level played
  • The date and time of the score

This data is publicly visible on the leaderboard. We do not collect or store your password, email address, or any other personally identifiable information as part of leaderboard submissions.

Account Data

If you create an account to participate in challenges, we store your username, a hashed password, email address, and country/region. Your password is hashed before storage and is never stored in plain text. Your email is used only for account recovery and challenge-related notifications.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data such as page views, session duration, and approximate geographic location. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users. You can opt out of Google Analytics by using browser privacy extensions or Do Not Track settings.

Cookies

Bean Boom uses localStorage for game settings and account data, not cookies. Google Analytics may set cookies to track anonymous usage statistics. We do not use cookies for advertising or third-party tracking.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare — Web hosting and content delivery
  • Google Analytics — Anonymous usage statistics
  • PayPal — Payment processing for paid challenges (if applicable)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Game data is stored locally in your browser. Server-side data (leaderboard scores, account information) is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Passwords are hashed before storage. All connections use HTTPS encryption.

Children's Privacy

Bean Boom does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game does not require registration to play, and leaderboard submissions only include a display name and score. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (stored locally in your browser)
  • Clear your data by clearing browser local storage
  • Request deletion of your leaderboard scores by contacting us
  • Request deletion of your account by contacting us

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page whenever we make changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
