About Bean Boom

A free online minesweeper game with a unique bead art style.

Bean Boom is a free browser-based minesweeper game that reimagines the classic puzzle with a colorful bead art aesthetic. Inspired by perler bead craft and nostalgic logic games, Bean Boom brings the timeless minesweeper experience to modern web browsers — no download, no registration, just instant play.

What Makes Bean Boom Different

While traditional minesweeper uses a flat grid of gray squares, Bean Boom replaces every cell with a colorful bead. The visual style is warm, tactile, and inviting — making the game approachable for new players while keeping the depth that minesweeper veterans love.

Three Difficulty Levels

Easy, Medium, and Hard boards offer challenges for every skill level, from first-timers to experts.

Global Leaderboard

Compete with players worldwide. Daily, monthly, and all-time rankings across all difficulties.

Bead Art Style

A unique visual identity inspired by perler bead craft. Colorful, warm, and instantly recognizable.

Daily Challenges

Paid challenges with full refunds on completion. Test your skills and earn your place on the board.

Cross-Platform

Works on desktop and mobile browsers. Touch controls for phones and tablets, mouse for desktop.

No Download Needed

Play instantly in your browser. No app store, no installation, no account required to start.

Design Philosophy

Bean Boom was built with a simple goal: make minesweeper fun and approachable again. Many online minesweeper games are bare-bones clones with no personality. We wanted to create a game that feels alive — with warm colors, satisfying interactions, and a sense of community through the global leaderboard.

The bead art style is central to this vision. It transforms the cold, abstract grid of classic minesweeper into something tactile and playful. Every revealed cell feels like popping a bead into place.

Technology

Bean Boom is a client-side web application built with modern web technologies. The game runs entirely in your browser, with leaderboard and challenge data stored on a serverless backend powered by Cloudflare Pages and Workers. This means fast load times, no server downtime, and a smooth gaming experience worldwide.
